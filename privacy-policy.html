BT Music – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

BT Music ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the BT Music mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

BT Music is an offline music player. The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play music stored on their devices.

The app may access audio files and related media information on your device to provide music playback features. This content is used for app functionality and is not uploaded to our servers by BT Music.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

BT Music uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, device information, advertising interactions and diagnostic data, subject to applicable settings, consent choices and Google's policies.

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), where applicable, to present privacy and advertising consent choices.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

3. Permissions

The app may request access to music or audio files on your device so that it can find and play your local music. Permissions are used only for the app's features.

4. Data Storage and Security

Music files and playback-related information are handled locally on your device. BT Music does not operate its own server to store your personal music library.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We encourage users to keep their devices protected.

5. Children's Privacy

BT Music is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may process data as described in Google's policies and applicable consent settings.

6. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Third-party services used by the app have their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies to understand how their information is handled.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
